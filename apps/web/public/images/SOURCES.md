| `membership-architecture-concept.webp` | Built-in ImageGen output (1536×1024), generated for the independent membership concept. Synthetic editorial architecture only: not a CSA venue, Erasmus University building, or identifiable Rotterdam landmark. No people, logo, lettering, or watermark.   |

The generated architectural image used built-in ImageGen in generation mode. Final prompt:

> Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: wide website membership hero background for a Chinese Student Association Rotterdam concept. Primary request: a sophisticated architectural study inspired by traditional Chinese pagoda roof eaves, transformed into a contemporary spatial composition rather than a literal travel photograph. Scene/backdrop: close architectural view of layered red lacquered timber brackets and dark ceramic roof tiles, with a subtle distant suggestion of Rotterdam glass-and-steel geometry through atmospheric haze. Subject: one elegant roofline sweeping diagonally into the frame; no people. Style/medium: cinematic editorial architectural photography blended with restrained matte-painted realism; refined, credible materials, subtle paper grain. Composition/framing: wide landscape; architectural detail concentrated on the right half and upper edge, generous deep-wine negative space on the left for interface copy, strong depth and crop tolerance for desktop and mobile. Lighting/mood: soft blue-hour light with warm red reflections, welcoming, cultured, contemporary, quietly dramatic. Color palette: CSA red, deep oxblood, warm off-white highlights, muted blue-gray atmosphere. Materials/textures: real wood grain, aged lacquer, ceramic tile, fine atmospheric grain. Constraints: no text, no letters, no logos, no emblems, no watermark, no people, no flags; do not depict a specific identifiable landmark; avoid tourist-poster clichés; avoid excessive ornamental clutter; keep the left side calm enough for high-contrast UI copy.

Poppins is CSA's public site typeface. The four Latin WOFF2 files under
`app/fonts/` are self-hosted copies from Google Fonts v24, licensed under the
SIL Open Font License 1.1.
